Where to launch the conversation

Source checks: 25 September 2026. These are candidate routes, not commitments, acceptances, endorsements, scheduled events or messages already sent. The goal is useful contribution from distinct domains, not the largest announcement count.

Recommended sequence

First: one canonical public prototype. Publish the approved introduction and versioned export on the existing research site, with a source repository or download, explicit contribution disclosure, examples and correction route. Archive a fixed release only after metadata and rights decisions are settled. Zenodo supports draft uploads, versioned records and software/GitHub integration; a reserved DOI is not registered until publication. Archive deposit does not constitute peer review. Upload guidance, software guidance.

Second: small domain clinics. Use the prepared domain cases to ask people to break a specific part of the model. Begin with contacts or venues the maintainer already has permission to approach; prepare organizer-facing pitches before any outreach. Each clinic should return a corrected example or a failure case, with facilitation and follow-up labor counted.

Third: scholarly artifacts shaped by those failures. A narrow formal paper, an implementation paper, a domain methods paper and a human study have different evidence requirements. Do not treat the entire dossier as one finished paper or split it into superficial venue-specific copies.

Specific community modalities to investigate

RDA Reproducibility Interest Group

Its remit includes reproducibility workflows, data/code connections and related infrastructure. Membership participation and contributions to sessions are described on its group page. A short session proposal should ask participants to compare the MCRP card with their existing records; do not request endorsement of the whole framework. No current conference deadline or speaking slot has been confirmed. Group and participation.

FORCE11 Upstream

A new, original perspective about what changes should invalidate reliance could fit its open-research scope. Its guidelines reject advertorials/product demos and describe an editorial contact workflow. Therefore pitch an argument with counterexamples, not the project-launch post copied verbatim. Disclose AI contributions and ask editors about fit before any submission. The page also expects engagement with comments after publication; budget that labor. Author guidelines.

OLS and research-transparency teaching communities

OLS is a possible open-science community connection; its current site redirects from Open Life Science to OLS. This is an audience-fit suggestion, not a verified open call. Use the biology exercise as a teaching contribution and inspect the current program before proposing a session. BITSS’s Catalyst program explicitly connects people advancing transparency and reproducibility in quantitative social science; a methods clinic is a plausible conversation, not a guaranteed program benefit. OLS, BITSS Catalysts.

SSRN and software publication

SSRN supports early research dissemination, but posting requires the relevant distribution rights and adherence to current terms. Confirm its current AI/editorial rules for the actual submission; copyright-policy review alone does not establish eligibility. Do not invent acceptance by an economics or law network. Rights guidance. JOSS is a later software-paper possibility; check its research-software scope and maturity expectations against actual reuse and documentation, not merely passing toy tests. JOSS guidance.

Routes to exclude from the immediate plan

  • OSF Preprints, including its community servers: current policy excludes mostly AI-generated content. This packet is not an eligible immediate upload. Policy.
  • bioRxiv as a general manifesto/protocol deposit: screening guidance excludes opinion/commentary and step-by-step protocols, and centers research with new data. Reassess only for a genuinely appropriate later study. Screening.
  • Broad automated mailing or social posting: no outreach has been authorized or performed in this task. Prepared copy is available for deliberate placement.
  • Claiming a standards body or domain community has adopted MCRP: discussion, indexing, a DOI and attendance do not establish adoption.

These are proposed milestones, not scheduled automations. Choose a realistic initial steward budget, e.g. four hours/week total, then reserve time for existing commitments before inviting more.
