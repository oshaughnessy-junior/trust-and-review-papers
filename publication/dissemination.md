# Where to launch the conversation

Source checks: 25 September 2026. These are candidate routes, not commitments,
acceptances, endorsements, scheduled events or messages already sent. The goal
is useful contribution from distinct domains, not the largest announcement count.

## Recommended sequence

**First: one canonical public prototype.** Publish the approved introduction and
versioned export on the existing research site, with a source repository or
download, explicit contribution disclosure, examples and correction route.
Archive a fixed release only after metadata and rights decisions are settled.
Zenodo supports draft uploads, versioned records and software/GitHub integration;
a reserved DOI is not registered until publication. Archive deposit does not
constitute peer review. [Upload guidance](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/create-new-upload/),
[software guidance](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/github/).

**Second: small domain clinics.** Use the prepared domain cases to ask people to
break a specific part of the model. Begin with contacts or venues the maintainer
already has permission to approach; prepare organizer-facing pitches before any
outreach. Each clinic should return a corrected example or a failure case, with
facilitation and follow-up labor counted.

**Third: scholarly artifacts shaped by those failures.** A narrow formal paper,
an implementation paper, a domain methods paper and a human study have different
evidence requirements. Do not treat the entire dossier as one finished paper or
split it into superficial venue-specific copies.

## Audience-specific surfaces

| Community | First surface and concrete hook | Contribution requested | Later route and condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physics / astronomy | Group methods seminar or collaboration software meeting; follow a calibration change through two downstream claims | One missing dependency and one over-broad reliance decision | Focused arXiv methods paper after human authorship, topicality and category/endorsement check; software indexing after applicable eligibility is established |
| Biology | Reproducibility journal club or open-science mentoring clinic; distinguish reruns from independent batches | A case where available evidence cannot justify transport or replication | Domain methods paper with real validation; bioRxiv only if the resulting work fits its research/new-data scope |
| Economics / social science | Methods workshop or replication seminar; compare group-first allocation and selective completion | A strategic counterexample and a credible labor measure | Working-paper series, SSRN or institutional RePEc route after human manuscript and rights review; do not assume direct RePEc upload |
| Law | Legal informatics / technology clinic; versioned authority and conflicts between scientific and legal handling | Identify authority, recipient and deadline errors in a synthetic dispute | A legal methods paper via an appropriate institutional series or SSRN after legal scholar review and current policy checks |
| Cross-domain infrastructure | Open research community discussion; the smallest record that survives correction | An interoperability adapter and an unnecessary field | Standards discussion after multiple independently maintained implementations and explicit governance |

arXiv submission is moderated, topical and author-account based, not assured by
an author's existing scientific reputation. Current guidance may require
endorsement for a new category. [Submission guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html).
RePEc is an aggregation ecosystem built around contributing archives and
metadata; an institutional series is the natural first discussion, not a
promise that this project can immediately post there. [RePEc](https://repec.org/),
[archive tutorial](https://ideas.repec.org/t/smap.html).

## Specific community modalities to investigate

### RDA Reproducibility Interest Group

Its remit includes reproducibility workflows, data/code connections and related
infrastructure. Membership participation and contributions to sessions are
described on its group page. A short session proposal should ask participants to
compare the MCRP card with their existing records; do not request endorsement of
the whole framework. No current conference deadline or speaking slot has been
confirmed. [Group and participation](https://www.rd-alliance.org/groups/reproducibility-ig/activity/).

### FORCE11 Upstream

A **new, original perspective** about what changes should invalidate reliance
could fit its open-research scope. Its guidelines reject advertorials/product
demos and describe an editorial contact workflow. Therefore pitch an argument
with counterexamples, not the project-launch post copied verbatim. Disclose AI
contributions and ask editors about fit before any submission. The page also
expects engagement with comments after publication; budget that labor.
[Author guidelines](https://upstream.force11.org/author-guidelines/).

### OLS and research-transparency teaching communities

OLS is a possible open-science community connection; its current site redirects
from Open Life Science to OLS. This is an audience-fit suggestion, not a verified
open call. Use the biology exercise as a teaching contribution and inspect the
current program before proposing a session. BITSS's Catalyst program explicitly
connects people advancing transparency and reproducibility in quantitative social
science; a methods clinic is a plausible conversation, not a guaranteed program
benefit. [OLS](https://we-are-ols.org/), [BITSS Catalysts](https://www.bitss.org/catalysts/).

### SSRN and software publication

SSRN supports early research dissemination, but posting requires the relevant
distribution rights and adherence to current terms. Confirm its current
AI/editorial rules for the actual submission; copyright-policy review alone does
not establish eligibility. Do not invent acceptance by an economics or law
network. [Rights guidance](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/copyright-policy).
JOSS is a later software-paper possibility; check its research-software scope
and maturity expectations against actual reuse and documentation, not merely
passing toy tests. [JOSS guidance](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html).

## Routes to exclude from the immediate plan

- **OSF Preprints, including its community servers:** current policy excludes
  mostly AI-generated content. This packet is not an eligible immediate upload.
  [Policy](https://help.osf.io/article/691-preprint-moderation-policies).
- **bioRxiv as a general manifesto/protocol deposit:** screening guidance excludes
  opinion/commentary and step-by-step protocols, and centers research with new
  data. Reassess only for a genuinely appropriate later study.
  [Screening](https://connect.biorxiv.org/news/2022/06/13/screening_procedures).
- **Broad automated mailing or social posting:** no outreach has been authorized
  or performed in this task. Prepared copy is available for deliberate placement.
- **Claiming a standards body or domain community has adopted MCRP:** discussion,
  indexing, a DOI and attendance do not establish adoption.

## A thirty-day launch sequence, relative to approval

| Phase | Deliverable | Success signal | Stop or narrow when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Approved page, versioned bundle, contribution route | Independent reader retrieves and runs the same release | Bytes or disclosure differ from approved candidate |
| Days 1–7 | Two small clinics in different domains | Participants produce understandable scoped records and concrete objections | Facilitation demand exceeds declared available hours |
| Days 8–14 | Public issue synthesis and corrected examples | Corrections link to affected versions and contributor credit is accurate | Sensitive disputes arrive without a capable operator |
| Days 15–21 | Third/fourth domain engagement and adapter attempt | At least one case challenges a central assumption | Only praise/downloads arrive; change the ask |
| Days 22–30 | Versioned response, narrowed next experiment | Clear decision on which mechanisms to keep, test or drop | Evidence supports the simple template just as well; simplify |

These are proposed milestones, not scheduled automations. Choose a realistic
initial steward budget, e.g. four hours/week total, then reserve time for existing
commitments before inviting more. The number is an example planning cap, not a
service promise. Report offered participation, completion, refusal, total labor,
new failure cases, repaired examples and unresolved issues. Downloads and stars
measure attention; they do not measure correct reliance or community adoption.
