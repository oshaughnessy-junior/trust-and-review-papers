# Bring one claim: the human path

You can try MCRP with a page of notes and a colleague. No account, token, trust
score, coding environment or new publication venue is needed for the exercise.
Use a synthetic case from this packet, or material you are entitled to discuss.
The proposed times below are facilitation budgets, not measured completion times.

You can start alone. Pick one row below and finish only its sentence; a colleague
is needed for the later paired exercise, not for this first contribution.

| Start here | One small action | A useful first sentence |
|---|---|---|
| [Physics / astronomy](../domains/physics-astro.md) | Divide the invented raw amplitude 10.5 by gain 1.00, then by 1.05 | “I reproduced 10.5 and 10.0; I did not validate the instrument calibration.” |
| [Biology](../domains/biology.md) | Compare the two control readings 9, 11 with the two treated readings 11, 13; notice that their batches differ too | “The observed means differ by two; this table does not separate treatment from batch.” |
| [Economics / social science](../domains/economics-social-science.md) | Weight the stipulated effects 2 and 0 by 0.8, 0.2, then by 0.2, 0.8 | “The two mixtures give 1.6 and 0.4; neither arithmetic check validates transport to a real population.” |
| [Law](../domains/law.md) | Schedule a three-tick task A due at tick 4 and a one-tick task B due at tick 1, both available now | “B then A meets both invented deadlines; I have not determined anyone's legal obligations.” |

These are worked starting answers, not endorsements. An equally useful response
is “I cannot tell what this sentence permits me to conclude,” with the confusing
phrase identified. No equation derivation, software installation or complete
domain chapter is required for that response.

## First contact: ten minutes

Pick a domain case and one intended use. Complete this card in ordinary language:

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| What is the one claim? | |
| Which version and evidence am I looking at? | |
| What one check would help with my intended use? | |
| What has not been checked? | |
| Who can decide whether this use is appropriate? | |
| What change would make us revisit that decision? | |

“Unknown” is an acceptable answer. It may prevent a particular use; it should not
prevent the person from contributing a useful partial check. Do not treat a
confidence slider as a substitute for these answers.

For example, an astronomy card could say: **claim:** the revised scalar estimate
is 10.0; **version/evidence:** teaching calculation v2, raw 10.5 and gain 1.05;
**check:** divide those numbers; **not checked:** physical calibration;
**decision maker:** I may choose it for my own synthetic classroom calculation,
but no real catalog authority has been supplied; **revisit if:** the raw value,
gain, or intended use changes. A “version” simply identifies the particular
copy being examined. A “dependency” is something that copy's claim uses, such as
the gain value. “Rely” means choosing a particular use, not declaring general truth.

## First contribution: a forty-five-minute paired exercise

One person plays the author and introduces the amendment; the other checks and
states a possible classroom use. Those combined teaching roles are not a model
of independent professional review. Use fictional role names if that is more
comfortable. A facilitator is optional; if nobody facilitates, record zero
facilitator time rather than inventing a third participant.

1. **Offer, 8 minutes.** The author supplies the card and a frozen, shareable
   evidence excerpt. The checker restates the intended use in their own words.
   If they disagree about the target, repair the wording before checking.
2. **Check, 12 minutes.** The checker performs one feasible action from the domain
   case, saves what was examined, and records passed, failed, partial or unknown.
   A timeout is partial or unknown, never an implicit pass.
3. **Rely, 8 minutes.** The designated exercise decision maker states a permitted
   toy use and its limits, or records that coverage is unavailable. This exercise
   designation grants no real scientific, institutional or legal authority.
4. **Amend, 10 minutes.** Introduce the case's dependency change. Both participants
   identify the exact prior uses that need reconsideration. Keep unaffected uses
   distinct and explain any uncertainty about the dependency map.
5. **Reflect, 7 minutes.** Count author, checker and facilitator time, missing
   evidence, unresolved disagreements, and unnecessary form fields. Compare with
   an ordinary referee paragraph on the same task.

The goal is a legible record and an honest limit. Correctly declining an
unsupported use is a successful exercise outcome.

## A facilitated domain clinic: seventy-five minutes

A facilitator prepares two comparable synthetic tasks, accessible materials,
and a short answer guide. Alternate which task receives ordinary prose review
and which receives the four-action card across pairs. Do not infer causal
efficacy from this small convenience exercise.

| Minutes | Activity | Tangible result |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Common example, scope and vocabulary | Shared intended use |
| 10–30 | First task in assigned format | Check and reliance record |
| 30–50 | Matched task in alternate format | Comparison record |
| 50–65 | Material amendment in each task | Affected-use map |
| 65–75 | Friction review | One counterexample and one simplification |

Capture all participants offered a task, all who started, all who completed,
refusals and reasons if voluntarily supplied. Include facilitator and repair
labor. A cleaner result among completers can hide exclusion. Provide an
asynchronous text option and permit withdrawal without penalty. Do not collect
identifiable observations for research or make evaluative claims about people
without the appropriate human-led ethics, consent and data-handling decisions.

## Progress through useful responsibilities

| Starting position | Useful contribution | Support needed | Evidence of readiness for more |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curious reader | Spot unclear scope in one card | Glossary and worked answer | Can distinguish check from decision |
| Domain participant | Complete a bounded check | Named mentor, evidence access | One reproducible scoped result |
| Experienced checker | Review a disagreement | Protected time and conflict disclosure | Explains competing interpretations fairly |
| Adapter author | Map an existing workflow | Domain checker and implementer | Preserves missing/unknown information |
| Steward candidate | Triage contributions and maintain records | Appointment, budget, alternate | Demonstrated response and correction process |

These are suggested contribution paths, not status ranks or automatic promotion.
Opportunity to learn requires supervision capacity. A newcomer reserve cannot
produce unavailable expertise. A contributor's reputation does not authorize
them to decide outside their remit.

## Contribution card

Send or file the following only in the destination the launch names. An issue
on a public repository is public; it is inappropriate for personal allegations,
patient data, confidential reviews or sensitive rights requests.

For a first contribution, the example/version, what you tried, and what confused
or failed are enough. The fuller card below is optional; “unknown” and blank
optional fields are acceptable. If no public feedback destination has been
announced yet, keep the note locally or discuss it with a colleague; the exercise
does not require submitting anything or finding a maintainer's personal address.

```text
Domain and intended use:
Example/claim version:
What I tried:
Expected behavior:
Observed behavior (include unknowns):
Smallest reproducing example, if shareable:
Suggested simplification or repair:
Time spent, including setup and follow-up:
Permission to quote this contribution: yes / no / ask
```

The launch must name an accountable intake owner, an alternate and the channels
they can actually service. Acknowledgment is not acceptance. Publish realistic
capacity and pause new discretionary commitments when capacity is exhausted.
Keep appropriate reporting routes for existing obligations reachable.
