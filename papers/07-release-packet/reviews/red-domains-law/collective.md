## Joint interpretation needed in the final release

**The component premises do not compose automatically.** The receipt runtime
trusts actor/control and authority declarations. The coupled model takes a
perceived audit probability as exogenous, without funding audits. The ecology
delivers a perfect audit oracle when stipulated access and capacity permit it.
The separate audit model has imperfect detection and an enforceable loss.
They expose different boundaries. Reading their favorable results together
cannot supply a single system with authenticated independent actors, real
sanctions, truthful science, funded monitoring and reliable notification.

That is a fruitful research gap, not a reason to block a public seed. An adapter
or future combined model should name which assumptions are replaced, which
remain external, and what happens when they disagree. For example, a biology
check can be a valid additive-model calculation while a reliance policy properly
refuses a causal interpretation. A legal operational decision can remain
unsupported while publication of a synthetic citation exercise is allowed.

**Recognition is not motivation evidence.** The ecology assigns credit for
specified events, including correction work. Its agents do not strategically
manufacture defects or volunteer according to recognition utility. Repair credit
can become a target for gaming, particularly when false alarms themselves create
repair tasks. The manuscript already excludes staged-repair incentives and does
not present a cooperation theorem. Keep that exclusion; test strategic credit
creation before interpreting the loop as a human incentive design.

**A quiet queue can be the wrong success signal.** The ecology's blind-control
case, the biology nonidentification case and the legal deadline example all
illustrate this differently. Unknown scientific defects, unreviewed causal
assumptions and unhandled obligations cannot be inferred absent from a small
queue. Report the ground-truth synthetic denominator in experiments and explicit
unknowns in actual records. Do not make adverse findings disappear by redefining
what counts as offered or unresolved work.

**Static publication and operating a service have different prerequisites.**
A corrected research artifact does not need a fully appointed appellate body or
proven human adoption before it can be discussed. It does need an honest byline,
rights disposition, exact candidate, reachable responsible contact and accurate
claims. Creating a live system that accepts third-party legal or sensitive
disputes adds obligations that the packet has not implemented. No adversarial
agent can grant those responsibilities by assent.
