## Claim audit rules

1. Replace `gap-source required`, `gap comparison required`, and `verification pending` only after recording a primary source, access date, exact supported feature, and reviewer initials.
2. A standards crosswalk supports compatibility claims; it does not support effectiveness claims.
3. A worked fixture supports `I` only for implemented representation or checks, never `E` for social or scientific outcomes.
4. If a claim cannot be falsified or bounded, narrow it or relabel it `H`.
5. Search the manuscript before G1 for: `first`, `solves`, `guarantees`, `trustworthy reviewer`, `proof of correctness`, `fully reproducible`, and unqualified `anonymous`.
6. Record negative and null findings. A failed proposed distinction is a result for the design map, not a reason to hide the test.
