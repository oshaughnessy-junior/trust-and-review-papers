## Human-evidence promotion rules

1. The final ethics determination and preregistration IDs must appear before any human-subject `E` claim is drafted.
2. Each supported `E` claim must name its estimand, population, comparison, uncertainty, data version, analysis run, deviations, and disclosure class.
3. Safety and privacy claims must identify the adversary, observation window, denominator, and unobserved threats; “no incidents observed” is not “safe.”
4. Qualitative claims must retain negative cases, investigator positionality, and domain/role heterogeneity while protecting participants.
5. If disclosure protection prevents independent inspection, state the limitation and provide a synthetic reproduction path plus controlled or attested verification where permitted.
6. A serious adverse event, protocol deviation, or later failed reproduction creates an explicit ledger event and may narrow or supersede a claim.
