## New derivations

All economics equations are the draft's explicitly specified toy models. Water filling is a conventional concave optimization result, not a novelty claim. The audit inequality is a two-action utility comparison. Queue formulas distinguish workload necessities, Little's relationship, and an M/M/1 illustration.
