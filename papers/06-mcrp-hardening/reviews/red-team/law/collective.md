## Cross-disciplinary conclusions

### Capacity is a joint necessary condition, not authority or a legal defense

Economics' finite reserve and science's shared-person ledger strengthen the law paper's impossibility result. Review, correction, incident triage, recipient-specific exports, standby, supervision, appeals, invitations and retries can all consume the same qualified hour. Reserving a lane assigns scarce service; it does not create it. Deadline and conflict constraints can make a plan infeasible even when total hours fit. Sponsor funding can pay a person but cannot establish their independence, grant their mandate or cause a founder to implement a reversal.

A narrow service is a reasonable next step. However, “already public” does not establish redistribution authority or remove privacy and jurisdiction questions. Reducing optional promises can reduce exposure and cost; it does not erase duties associated with actual conduct. These are concrete launch facts to determine, not grounds to block completion of this analytical dossier.

### Emergency containment is both a remedy and an attack surface

I accept games' illustrative strategic decomposition:

`expected attacker gain = containment probability × duration × attacker benefit rate + diversion benefit − submission cost − expected enforceable loss`.

It is a diagnostic, not calibrated behavioral law. Eventual merits defeat need not eliminate the value of a temporary takedown near a publication, grant or hiring deadline. Reversible actions can have irreversible opportunity costs. Depth limits on ordinary appeals do not deter a strategy that succeeds during provisional containment.

The safeguard must not equate losing a complaint, imposing workload, or remaining anonymous with abuse. A lawful good-faith report may be inconvenient and unproved. The next simulation should distinguish malicious relabeling, genuine urgent harm, uncertain facts and harmless ordinary disputes; measure false-containment duration, missed urgent harm and staff labor. Authority to contain, expiry/review and current-use consequences belong in that trace. Do not manufacture a universal classifier or penalty regime from the payoff expression.

### Historical truth claims and current permission to rely must remain different

Science's currentness repair is compatible with legal coordinate separation. Removing indispensable evidence can make current reliance unsupported while leaving the old scientific disposition historically intact. Notification and correction requirements are separate from the impossibility of recalling all copies. Offline authenticity does not prove present availability or legal permission to disclose.

The renamed unresolved-change field is better than a latest-ever-change flag. A new authorized receipt can incorporate an amendment; later unresolved evidence can reopen reconsideration. The helper deliberately trusts fixture facts and is not an event-authorization or live freshness implementation. It should not be credited with ensuring that the change was actually adjudicated or that downstream readers received it.

### Opportunity and completion are different denominators

I agree with all peers that a newcomer reserve is conditional access to feasible resources, not unconditional completed scientific review. Supervisory labor must be charged, and unavailable independent expertise cannot be substituted silently. Report offered, eligible, invited, accepted, completed and relied-upon work, refusals/retries and relevant strata. Better accuracy among easy completions is not evidence of better whole-service performance. Unassessed work is not thereby false.

Games' representative-cloning and selective-completion counterexamples matter institutionally as well as mathematically: a procedurally valid panel can still be unfairly sampled, and a valid offer distribution can become distorted among actual decisions. Law supplies no safe shortcut around this with a responsibility label or affiliation check.
