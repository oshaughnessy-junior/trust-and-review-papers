## Blocking conditions versus research-only issues

**Before an operational/contested human pilot:** establish a lawful recipient-specific disclosure process; keep protected reporting reachable in degraded mode; obtain a real independent appeal route with record access and effective reversal authority; debit staff once across roles and retries; preserve currentness/unknown status; define and test the chosen sampling boundary; authenticate the facts that the toy boundary projection trusts. A newcomer or emergency reserve is a scheduling rule, not a creation of skill-hours or legal authority.

**Before describing a numerical helper as reusable:** close economics' solver-range and invalid-input findings with genuine regressions. This is separate from whether the conditional water-filling theorem is correct. Economics' collective lane should adjudicate the latest solver implementation.

**Research-only at present:** incentive selection, effort-monitor discrimination, group identity quality, emergency-label behavior, calibrated scientific loss, withheld dependency discovery, finite resource fairness, dynamic repair regimes, human uptake and external interoperability. These need explicit experiments or institutional arrangements, not another layer of reassuring status fields.

**Not blockers to a clearly labeled private research dossier:** incomplete general equilibrium, inability to compel volunteers, unresolved broad fairness, or lack of human uptake measurements. Retain them as conspicuous limits; do not imply their absence proves failure of the small interface.

## Major unmodeled deviations for the pilot action ledger

- Claim/bundle fragmentation, group splitting/merging, and strategic capability declarations.
- Preemptive identity reset, parallel accounts, credential lending and mandate expiry avoidance.
- Audit refusal, fabricated completion, seed grinding, suppressed receipts and selective completion.
- Bribing, threatening or economically conditioning an auditor, steward, appeal officer or sponsor-dependent operator.
- Emergency relabeling, targeted temporary containment, rights-intake flooding and serial plausible new evidence.
- Hiding scientific dependencies, strategically narrowing check scope, and cherry-picking easily completed evidence.
- Delaying correction until a useful deadline passes; relying on authentic stale exports.
- Staged error/correction to farm credit; transferring risk to an insolvent or unreachable principal.
- Manipulating queue scope or funding withdrawal without touching the verdict function.

For emergency routing, an illustrative attack payoff is p_contain × duration × harm_rate + diversion_benefit − submission_cost − expected_enforceable_loss. Eventual merits defeat does not imply negative payoff. This is a proposed hypothesis to measure, not a calibrated law or justification for penalizing lawful reporting. Likewise, no fixed list constitutes universal deviation coverage.
