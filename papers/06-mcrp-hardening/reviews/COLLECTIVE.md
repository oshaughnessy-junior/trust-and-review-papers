# Collective adversarial assessment

**Common statement reviewed by four dedicated adversarial lanes, 25 September
2026.** Final lane verdicts are recorded below. This report is
separate from the author's response in `ADJUDICATION.md`.

## Common recommendation

Retain the package as a **bounded analytical research dossier and private prototype
hardening contribution**. Do not present it as an operationally validated review
commons, an equilibrium solution, a guarantee of current scientific reliability,
legal clearance, or an externally reviewed publication.

The strongest defensible thesis is that small, version-bound reliance records can
make assumptions, responsibility, and repair more inspectable. The mathematics
and counterexamples expose conditions under which this might work and ways it can
fail. They do not show that participants will use it, that all dependencies will
be discovered, or that the institutions needed to enforce it already exist.

The conditional KKT allocation result, fixed-model stationary influence bound,
local feedback eigenvalue, covariance identity, homogeneous and multitype

and limited coalition comparison survive within their stated assumptions.
None is a new universal theorem about peer review. Counterexamples remain central

The ordinary numerical regressions identified by economics were repaired and

## Joint findings that no single discipline could settle

1. **One hour can be spent only once.** Review, audit, repair, supervision, intake,
   rights handling and appeal draw on a shared person/skill/epoch budget. Money,
   graph reachability and a named alternate cannot create absent independent
   expertise. A newcomer reserve offers a conditional opportunity, not guaranteed
   completed service.
2. **Every selection stage changes the population.** Offers, eligibility,
   invitations, acceptances, completions and actual reliance need separate
   denominators, including refusal, exclusion, rerolls and unresolved work.
   A smaller queue or a higher accuracy among completers can result from rejected
   work rather than better science. Retry and reserved-standby costs belong in
   the same budget.
3. **Local records do not control remote consequences.** Authentic historical
   decisions can be stale, removed evidence can undermine present use, and
   recipient copies can outlive local deletion. Currentness needs explicit
   receipt-relative unresolved-change semantics and a declared refresh policy.
   An amendment may later be resolved by new authorized reliance.
4. **Authority must be executable.** An appeal reviewer needs access, protected
   resources, recusal rules and a way to implement reversal. Private statutory
   handling may require transmission to an opposing party. A compact interface
   must reveal these consequential boundaries rather than hide them.
5. **Adversaries can target the service, not merely its verdict.** Strategic
   refusal, task rerolling, false emergencies, staged repair credit, hidden control,
   selective evidence, author-driven contract weakening and audit suppression
   lie outside simple one-step games. An emergency label can buy containment time
   or divert labor even when ordinary appeal depth is bounded.
6. **More mathematical structure is not automatically more useful.** Unknown
   utility estimates can make optimized allocation worse than a simple policy.
   A succession of stable snapshot models can be dynamically unstable. The first
   experiment should compare the four-action interface with a plain structured
   template and count total human labor, before adding elaborate trust routing.

## Gates still open for a contested live pilot

- Actual accountable operator, applicable legal duties, recipient disclosures,
  deadline-aware obligations, reachable degraded reporting and wind-down plan.
- Real appointment, funding, independence and reversal authority for appeals.
- Authenticated identities and assertions, common-control assessment, privacy
  boundaries, and live currentness/update infrastructure.
- Complete-panel scheduling and distribution-level representation invariance,
  with completion/retry semantics and shared resource reservations.
- Observations of missing dependencies, unknown scope, hidden common causes,
  subgroup exclusion and total labor from an appropriately approved pilot.

These are operational gates, not reasons to suppress a transparently conditional
research draft. The ordinary numerical solver's extreme floating-point failures
remain documented, explicit errors rather than silently returned allocations.
No adversarial lane supplies missing human, legal or institutional authority.

## Deliberation and independence

Economics, games and science exchanged direct cross-questions during the collective
round. Messages to the law lane encountered an active-agent limit; the integrator
relayed them and activated law for its response. Each lane's individual report,
collective contribution, and final verdict are retained. The shared model/operator
context prevents calling this external peer review or independent institutional
validation. Agreement is recorded only when a lane explicitly gives it; any
dissent or condition remains visible.
