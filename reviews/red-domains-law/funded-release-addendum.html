Funded-audit interpretation

The experiment closes the promised-versus-executed inspection gap within its toy environment; it does not close the auditor-effort problem. The manuscript states that auditors perform scheduled work with stipulated detection behavior. Its final boundary explicitly retains strategic auditor refusal/shirking, false reports, dependence and appeal reversal as unmodeled. Neither the claim map nor the synthesis upgrades funded execution into observed human diligence.

The eight output “actors” are now clearly eight task decision units, potentially mapped to fewer execution people. Separate utilities, task-specific escrow and pre-reserved capacity are premises, not evidence of eight independent reviewers. The concealed marginal calculation needs the fixed population, information timing and participating decision’s independence from the subset; the prose states those requirements. Disclosure and partial-refusal controls make their importance visible. A future shared-person or coalition model cannot silently inherit independent decision behavior from the labels.

Tokens, hours and utility effort are distinguished. The escrow transfer is collectible only inside the fixture. It establishes no real asset, deposit policy, consent, lawful fine or legitimate disciplinary process. The text explicitly omits locked-collateral opportunity cost and nonlinear wealth effects. Therefore it cannot show equitable participation across resource levels, and does not claim to. Describing insufficient collateral as pre-offer unavailability is honest within the model; it is not a recommendation to exclude actual poor contributors.

The shared-role reservation is deliberately conservative and its unused slack is reported. Refusal/triage labor is zero only in this experiment and marked as an omission. The coupled queue separately charges it; assembling both results does not automatically create one integrated funded, deadline-aware system. Rights, recusal, lawful recipient disclosure and remedies remain institutional questions. No additional legal disclaimer or operating institution is needed merely to publish this carefully limited synthetic experiment.
