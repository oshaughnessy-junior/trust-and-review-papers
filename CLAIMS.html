Release-packet contribution and claim map

This is a research-prototype packet. It supplies an auditable argument and reproduction objects for the working publication sequence; it does not declare all planned papers submission-ready. The claim labels use the repository’s science-writing vocabulary: P prior work, D design, T conditional theory, I implementation/synthetic experiments, E empirical evidence, H open hypothesis. No human-subject or field efficacy result is claimed here.

Research questions and bounded answers

Manuscript Question Answer supported by this packet
Synthesis Can a small shared boundary support work across different internal organizations? The proposed boundary can be specified and exercised; human benefit and adoption remain open.
Mathematical foundations Which allocation, completion, repair and audit guarantees survive explicit assumptions? Four conditional models yield exact bounds and counterexamples; their premises must not be combined silently.
Coupled agents Do local allocation properties survive capacity, completion and reliance selection? The retained synthetic pipeline exhibits failures across those stages under one shared person ledger.
Local rules Can a small recognition loop improve attention allocation without entrenchment or gaming? It has a conditional attention ceiling, but retained tradeoffs and a profitable correction-credit deviation prevent a general incentive claim.
Funded audits Does an announced inspection probability correspond to funded delivered inspection? A fixed-population toy can deliver concealed quota marginals under stated resource/information premises; broken promises, early disclosure and false sanctions defeat broader interpretations.

Methods, negative controls and review status

Each experiment states its fixed assumptions, comparison policies, negative cases and interpretation of uncertainty. The root reproduction command retains failures as evidence and records code, fixture, table and saved-result hashes. The reproduction guide distinguishes core tests from optional renderers. The collective report records adversarial findings and dispositions rather than treating another agent’s agreement as proof.

G0-G3 have internal argument, scholarship, methods and artifact evidence at a prototype level, with the limitations stated above. Separate AI drafting/review lanes do not replace the repository’s independent-review and human-release requirements. G4/G5 are not claimed passed for journal submission or operational use. The static protocol seed has a narrower release question; the user requested its preparation and readiness assessment, not an invented record of human approval.

Stop an extended claim when its needed premise is missing. Prefer a simpler structured record or uniform assignment if the additional mechanism has no worthwhile advantage. Those are research and design stopping rules, not penalties for contributors who refuse work or disclose an honest correction.

Agent-first release extension

The agent entry contract adds a bounded local JSON interface and machine-readable discovery. This is an implementation result (I) and design choice (D), not evidence of agent identity, real independence or human efficacy. The full offered profile binds claim/evidence, scope, resolved dependencies and required coverage; a metadata-only change therefore has a distinct version. Aliases resolve through a separately loaded trusted policy to one principal’s ledger. Conditional alias invariance requires correct principal mapping and does not discover undisclosed common control.

Author and independent adversarial suites exercise those boundaries. Four domain traces preserve old pending reliance while establishing reliance on a changed version. The agent launch critique records its own findings and limitations. The integration runner includes these suites, input fixtures and regenerated outputs in its validation inventory.
